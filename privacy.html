Privacy Notice

How personal information submitted through this website is collected, used, shared and protected.

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Elizabeth Salmon-James (“the practice,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information gathered through this website.

Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, the practice area you select, and the details of your message. We do not use cookies or other technologies to track visitors for advertising or analytics purposes, and we do not collect personal information automatically beyond standard web server logs (such as IP address and browser type) that most websites generate.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry and, where appropriate, to provide legal services you have requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on your consent, given when you voluntarily submit it through the contact form, and where necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a client relationship.

How We Share Information

Contact form submissions are processed through our website hosting provider's form-handling service solely to deliver your message to us. We do not share your information with any other third party except as required by law or as necessary to provide legal services you have engaged us for.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or, if you become a client, in accordance with our professional and legal record-keeping obligations.

Your Rights

Under Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your information (subject to any legal or professional obligations to retain it), and withdraw your consent to our processing of your information at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with Jamaica's Information Commissioner if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information submitted through this website, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice or wish to exercise any of your rights, please contact us at info@salmonlawoffices.com or esalmon@salmonlawoffices.com, or by mail at 2A Strathairn Avenue, Kingston 10, Jamaica.
