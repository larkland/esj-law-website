About the attorney

Experienced counsel, personally delivered

Elizabeth L. Salmon-James holds a Bachelor of Arts in History from the University of the West Indies and both a Bachelor of Laws (Honours) and a Master of Laws from the University of Liverpool. She was called to the Bar of England & Wales in 2003 as a Barrister and is a member of the Honourable Society of Lincoln's Inn.

Admitted to the Jamaican Bar in 2004, she practised as a Legal Officer in the insurance sector before joining the litigation department of Rattray Patterson Rattray in 2007, where she was admitted as an Equity Partner in 2012. Since 2019 she has practised as a sole practitioner. She was commissioned as a Notary Public for the Island of Jamaica in 2017.
