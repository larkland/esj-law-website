Elizabeth L. Salmon-James Attorney-at-Law & Notary Public
Profile

Professional background

Mrs. Salmon-James holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in History from the University of the West Indies, together with a Bachelor of Law (Honours) degree and a Master's Degree in Law, both from the University of Liverpool. She was called to the Bar of England & Wales in 2003 as a Barrister and is a member of the Honourable Society of Lincoln's Inn.

She was admitted to practise as an Attorney-at-Law at the Jamaican Bar in 2004 and shortly thereafter worked as a Legal Officer with United General Insurance Company, which later became Advantage General Insurance Company. In 2007 she joined the Litigation Department of the firm Rattray Patterson Rattray as an Associate, and on 1 January 2012 she was admitted as an Equity Partner. In 2019, Mrs. Salmon-James transitioned to becoming a sole practitioner and has been acting in that capacity ever since. In 2017 she was commissioned as a Notary Public for the Island of Jamaica and is a member of the Society of Notaries Public.

Mrs. Salmon-James is a member of the Jamaican Bar Association. Her main areas of practice include insurance law — particularly insurance defence litigation — civil litigation, personal injury litigation, contract, employment law, landlord and tenant, debt collection and conveyancing, amongst other areas. She has appeared both as Counsel and as an instructing Attorney instructing Senior Counsel in various litigation matters before the Parish Courts, the Supreme Court of Judicature of Jamaica and the Court of Appeal. She has also appeared in various court-ordered mediations and has advised and prepared opinions for security companies, media houses and insurance companies. She is on the panel of attorneys of insurance and security companies.

Professional philosophy

Mrs. Salmon-James offers personalized legal services to corporate and individual clients in an efficient, responsive and cost-effective manner. As a sole practitioner, she handles each matter directly — the attorney who assesses a case is the attorney who conducts it — bringing partner-level judgment and courtroom experience to every engagement, whether appearing as Counsel or instructing Senior Counsel.
