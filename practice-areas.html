Insurance Law

Insurance law — and in particular insurance defence litigation — has been central to Mrs. Salmon-James's practice since she began her career as a Legal Officer in the sector. She acts on the panel of attorneys for insurance companies, defends claims through to trial and appeal, and prepares coverage and liability opinions.

  • Defence of motor, liability and property claims
  • Coverage analysis and policy interpretation opinions
  • Panel representation and advice to insurers

Civil Litigation

Mrs. Salmon-James has conducted civil disputes before the Parish Courts, the Supreme Court of Judicature of Jamaica and the Court of Appeal, appearing both as Counsel and as an instructing attorney instructing Senior Counsel. She also represents clients in court-ordered mediation.

  • Claims and defences in the Parish and Supreme Courts
  • Appeals to the Court of Appeal
  • Court-ordered and private mediation

Personal Injury Litigation

Acting for claimants and for defendants and their insurers, Mrs. Salmon-James handles personal injury claims arising from road traffic accidents, workplace incidents and public liability, from initial assessment through negotiation, trial and, where necessary, appeal.

  • Motor vehicle accident claims
  • Workplace and occupiers' liability claims
  • Assessment of quantum and settlement negotiation

Contract

Advice on the formation, interpretation and enforcement of commercial and personal contracts, including drafting and review of agreements and the conduct of contractual disputes.

  • Drafting and review of commercial agreements
  • Breach of contract claims and defences
  • Advice on rights, remedies and risk

Employment Law

Representation and advice for employers and employees on the employment relationship, from contracts and workplace policies to disciplinary processes, redundancy and claims for unjustified dismissal.

  • Contracts of employment and workplace policies
  • Disciplinary hearings and redundancy processes
  • Termination and dismissal disputes

Landlord & Tenant

Advice and litigation for landlords and tenants of residential and commercial premises, including the preparation of leases, recovery of possession, rent arrears and disputes over repairs and covenants.

  • Preparation and review of leases and tenancy agreements
  • Recovery of possession and arrears
  • Breach of covenant and dilapidations disputes

Debt Collection

Recovery of outstanding sums for businesses and individual creditors, from letters of demand through the issue of proceedings and enforcement of judgment.

  • Letters of demand and pre-action correspondence
  • Claims for liquidated sums and default judgment
  • Enforcement of judgments

Conveyancing

Handling the sale, purchase and transfer of land and residential property, including review of title, preparation of agreements for sale and transfer instruments, and mortgage documentation.

  • Agreements for sale and transfers of land
  • Title review and requisitions
  • Mortgage and security documentation

Notarial Services

As a Notary Public commissioned for the Island of Jamaica since 2017 and a member of the Society of Notaries Public, Mrs. Salmon-James notarises, authenticates and certifies documents for use both locally and overseas.

  • Notarisation and certification of documents and copies
  • Administration of oaths, affirmations and statutory declarations
  • Authentication of documents for use abroad

Advisory & Legal Opinions

Beyond the courtroom, Mrs. Salmon-James advises and prepares written opinions for security companies, media houses and insurance companies, and has served on corporate and government boards. She offers ongoing counsel to corporate clients on legal risk and strategy.

  • Written legal opinions and risk assessments
  • Retainer advice for corporate and institutional clients
  • Board-level and governance guidance
